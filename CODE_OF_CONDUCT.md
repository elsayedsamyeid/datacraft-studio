# Code of conduct

This repository is where people report bugs, ask for features and help each other use
DataCraft Studio. To keep it useful and welcoming:

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Keep reports focused, specific and on topic.
- Help others when you can, and credit their help.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal or political attacks.
- Posting anyone's private information, including email addresses, credentials or data.
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly bumping issues.
- Sharing ways to bypass Pro licensing, or requesting cracked copies.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or lock comments and issues, and block accounts that break
these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or
issue. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
