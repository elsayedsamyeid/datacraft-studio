# Pro and accounts

DataCraft Studio is **one app for everyone**. Free needs no account and never asks you to
sign in. Signing in with a DataCraft Pro account unlocks the Pro features in the same app.

## What Pro adds

- Visual query builders for SQL, MongoDB and Elasticsearch
- Query history and a saved workspace
- Guided data import wizard
- Data migration between engines
- Provider health checks
- AI assistant with your choice of model provider

In Free, these show as **Requires Pro** under **Settings → Features**.

## Getting Pro

Registering with your email creates a Free account. DataCraft enables Pro on that account after a Pro purchase is arranged. Registration alone does not unlock Pro.

1. Open **Settings → Features → Plan** and choose **Sign in to unlock Pro**.
2. The sign-in page opens in your **web browser**. Sign in, or create an account, and
   **verify your email**—an unverified email can't sign in.
3. [Open a Pro request](https://github.com/elsayedsamyeid/datacraft-studio/issues/new?template=pro_request.yml).
   Issues are public: give your email **partly hidden** (for example `s****@gmail.com`) and the
   day you signed in. Never post a password.
4. After DataCraft enables Pro on your account, **sign out and sign in again**.
   **Settings → Features → Plan** shows **Pro**, and the Pro features unlock.

DataCraft never sees your password. The browser signs you in and hands the app a signed proof
of your plan, which the app verifies itself.

## The one-computer licence

A Pro licence is for **one person on one computer at a time**.

- **Moving to another computer:** sign in there. The licence moves, and the previous computer
  is signed out the next time it checks in.
- **Moving back and forth very often** within a short period is blocked, to stop accounts being
  shared. If you hit the limit, sign out on the other computer, or open a Pro request.
- **Freeing a computer straight away:** open your profile and choose **Sign out this device**.

A copied sign-in doesn't work on a second computer, and copying the app's files to another
machine gives Free there.

## Working offline

- Pro checks your plan when the app starts and every few hours while you're online.
- Without internet, Pro keeps working for **up to three days** since the last successful check.
- After that the app runs as Free until it can reach the account service again. Nothing is
  lost—reconnect and Pro returns.
- Changing the computer's clock does not extend the offline period.

## Signing in again

For security, a sign-in expires after a set period even if you use the app daily. When it
does, the app returns to Free until you sign in again from **Settings → Features → Plan**.

## Signing out

Open your profile and choose **Sign out this device**. This:

- ends the session and frees the licence for another computer;
- removes the sign-in data from this computer;
- **keeps** your connections, queries, workspace and settings.

## Privacy

Signing in sends your email, name and an anonymous device identifier to the account service—
never your databases, queries or connection details. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
